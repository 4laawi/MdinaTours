# Mdina Tours Content Rules

Whenever working on Mdina Tours private-driver pages, booking content, FAQs, SEO copy or related customer-facing content:

1. Read `/docs/PRIVATE_DRIVER_SERVICE_TRUTH.md` first.
2. Treat it as the operational source of truth.
3. Never invent policies, inclusions, vehicle guarantees or service details.
4. Prefer improving existing sections instead of adding new ones.
5. Keep conversion pages concise.
6. Human operational knowledge should be preserved, not replaced with generic AI copy.
7. Do not add content merely to increase SEO word count.
8. If a fact is not confirmed, either omit it or ask before implementing it.
9. Maintain consistency between:
   - booking widget
   - FAQs
   - fleet
   - inclusions
   - schema
   - meta copy
   - city-specific private driver pages
10. Never change approved UI/design while performing content or SEO optimization unless explicitly requested.
