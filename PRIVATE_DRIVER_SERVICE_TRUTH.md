# Mdina Tours — Private Driver Service Truth

This file contains verified operational information about Mdina Tours private driver services.

Treat these facts as the source of truth when writing website copy, FAQs, booking interfaces, SEO content, ads, or customer-facing text.

Do not invent details that are not listed here.

---

## Driver Role

Mdina Tours drivers are professional drivers.

Their primary job is transportation and safe driving.

Drivers are happy to:
- help passengers with luggage
- provide practical local recommendations
- suggest useful stops
- provide general guidance during the journey

Drivers are NOT professional tour guides.

If a customer wants a professional licensed guide for sightseeing, monuments, medinas, or historical explanations, a guide can be arranged separately for an additional affordable fee.

Do not describe drivers as tour guides.

---

## Medina / Difficult Access Pickups

Some riads and accommodations inside Moroccan medinas cannot be reached directly by vehicle.

In these situations, the driver will get the customer as close as reasonably possible to the accommodation using the nearest vehicle-accessible location.

Drivers always do their best to assist customers with their luggage.

Do not promise that every riad or property can be reached directly by car.

---

## Full-Day / Mise à Disposition Flexibility

A full-day private driver service is flexible.

Customers can normally:
- request photo stops
- stop for coffee
- stop for lunch
- make comfort stops
- spend more time at places they enjoy
- make reasonable changes during the day

For local/city-based mise à disposition, the schedule can usually remain highly flexible.

For trips involving multiple cities or significant route changes, customers should inform Mdina Tours.

Significant itinerary changes that affect:
- distance
- driving time
- route
- additional cities

may result in an additional fee.

Do not claim that every itinerary change is always free.

---

## Multi-Day Trips

Mdina Tours needs the complete planned itinerary in advance in order to quote a multi-day journey correctly.

The customer does NOT separately pay for the driver's operating expenses.

The quoted rate includes the driver's required travel expenses.

Customers should not be asked to separately pay for:
- driver's accommodation
- driver's operating expenses
- fuel
- tolls
- normal parking costs

These costs are handled by Mdina Tours as part of the agreed service price.

The customer should provide the full itinerary so the correct quote can be calculated.

## Vehicle Selection

Vehicle choice depends mainly on:
- number of passengers
- amount of luggage
- comfort requirements

For one or two passengers, a sedan or SUV is generally sufficient.

For multiple passengers, especially when traveling with luggage, a van or Mercedes Vito becomes more practical.

Vans generally offer:
- more passenger space
- more luggage space
- more room in the rear cabin
- improved comfort for groups and longer journeys

Do not imply that a customer is guaranteed a specific exact vehicle model unless availability has been confirmed.

Equivalent vehicles may be used depending on availability.

## Payment Policy

Mdina Tours accepts:

Currencies:
- EUR
- USD
- Moroccan dirhams (MAD)

Payment methods:
- cash
- card

Payment is made AFTER EACH TRAVEL DAY.

Do not describe the policy as:
- payment at pickup
- full payment before travel

unless a specific booking requires otherwise.

Mdina Tours values transparent pricing.

The customer should know the agreed price before the service takes place.

---

## Pricing Transparency

The customer should not encounter unexpected operating charges after agreeing to the quoted price.

For an agreed itinerary, normal service costs such as fuel, tolls and standard operating expenses are incorporated into the quotation.

If the customer significantly changes the itinerary, route, distance or scope of service, an additional fee may apply.

Any such change should be communicated transparently.
